# Event Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 21 September 2026  
**Event:** Tshwane Varsity Hackathon (and any replay of this ElectroRaid demo at a City or campus event)

This notice covers **personal information collected because you are at the event**, not the fake CIS records inside the app. For in-app data see [Privacy & Cookie Policy](../PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and [Visitor Privacy](VISITOR_PRIVACY.md).

## 1. Organisers

The hackathon organisers and, where they host a booth, the City of Tshwane. ElectroRaid authors process demo-booth interactions only as described here.

## 2. What an event may collect

| Activity | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration | Name, campus, email, team name | Judging, Wi-Fi, certificates |
| Booth demo | The persona you tap (Sibusiso, Thandiwe, Sipho, Nomsa) | Show the four municipal roles |
| Photos / video | Stage, booth, screens | Event recap — **do not film residents’ real accounts** |
| Q&A | Questions you ask judges or the team | Improve the pitch |

The ElectroRaid process does **not** receive the hackathon registration list. Login is local to the browser.

## 3. Live demo hygiene

- Use only seeded accounts in `src/lib/seed.ts`.  
- If a visitor types a real street address into **Other**, treat it as event data: restart the app after the session.  
- Do not leave a dispatcher session unlocked on a public screen.

## 4. Retention

Event organisers keep registration data under their own notice. ElectroRaid demo state is wiped on reset or process restart. Pitch-deck screenshots should crop any visitor-typed notes.

## 5. Recordings

If the organisers livestream the pitch, that stream is their processing. Ask them — not this repo — for takedown of your face or voice.

## 6. Contact

Hackathon information desk, or the City contact on the event brief. ElectroRaid privacy: [Privacy & Cookie Policy](../PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
