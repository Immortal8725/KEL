# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective:** 21 September 2026  
**Product:** GridPulse

This policy sits under the [Terms of Use](../TERMS_OF_USE.md). It applies to every role in [CODE.md](../CODE.md).

## Allowed

- Demonstrate outage reporting, anonymous tips, dispatch, field close-out, inspector QA, and ROI with **seeded or invented demo data**.  
- Reset the ops floor between judging runs.  
- Export or screenshot the **plain-language audit diary** for the pitch.  
- Run the app locally or on the hackathon preview host.

## Not allowed

- Enter **real** resident ID numbers, live prepaid tokens, or unpublished employee home addresses.  
- Use an anonymous tip to harass a neighbour, a political opponent, or a child.  
- Issue or collect a **real** tamper fine from this UI.  
- Attempt to break the SHA-256 chain, inject XSS via notes, or scan other tenants (there are none — do not try).  
- Treat Izinyoka flags as a finding of guilt. The code only flags 0 kWh ≥ 60 days on an ENERGIZED feeder (`src/lib/engines/anomaly.ts`).  
- Impersonate City officials outside the four demo persona cards.  
- Scrape OpenStreetMap tiles in a way that breaches OSM tile usage policy.  
- Upload malware, or use the field evidence endpoint to store anything other than the demo SVG/signature payloads.

## Enforcement

We may reset the store, disable a preview, or report unlawful content to the City or SAPS. Repeat abuse of anonymous tips will be treated as a material breach of the Terms.

## Reporting misuse

Tell a team member or the hackathon organisers. For a formal complaint about personal information, use the [Dispute Policy](DISPUTE_POLICY.md).
